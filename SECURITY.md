# Security policy

Earthward AI runs a public site, so we take reports seriously and answer them quickly.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **ved@earthwardai.com** with a description, the steps to reproduce it and the impact you think it has. Please don't open a public issue or test against other people's data. We'll reply within three working days and keep you posted until it's fixed.

In scope: earthwardai.com and the code in this repository. Prompt injection that gets past the council's rules (the 30% cap, the per-visitor limits, or the quoting of donor notes) counts, and we'd like to hear about it.

Out of scope: denial of service, automated scanner output without a working example, and missing headers that have no practical impact.
